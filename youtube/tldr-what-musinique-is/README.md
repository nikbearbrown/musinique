# What Musinique Is (tldr film)

**Title:** What Musinique Is · **Author:** Nik Bear Brown (narrated by Liam, a synthetic Kokoro voice, in for Bear) · **Status:** see `BUILD-LOG.md`. Not published.

**What it is.** A `tldr` overview of Musinique: an independent label, and a free toolkit being built for indie musicians. One artist's week after a finished song is the running case. The machine takes the chores (clean titles, release files) and offers lyric drafts in an artist's voice; the artist sings, hears and rewrites them into the final lyrics. A playlist offer a scanner calls clean is checked against the artist's own data and vetoed, and the budget survives. The rule arrives last: the machine executes, you decide, on your own data. Brand and copy go to Madison, video to brutalist.art.

**Why.** Bear asked for an overview in the Claude colors and the default voice (2026-09-23).
