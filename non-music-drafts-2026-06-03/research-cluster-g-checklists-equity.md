- The WACC checklist correctly presents the formula and explains the tax advantage mechanism for debt.
- The mezzanine financing checklist correctly identifies the 20-30% return expectation and notes that "many financial experts believe that this type of financing has aggravated the recent credit crunch" — an interesting observation that receives no elaboration.
- The SWF profiles are dated (2011 estimates) but were accurate at time of publication. Asset sizes have shifted substantially since.
- The sovereign wealth fund strategies checklist correctly identifies the passive/strategic investment distinction and the transparency problem.

**Methodological Soundness:** Adequate as reference. The WACC and MM checklists are the most technically precise.
