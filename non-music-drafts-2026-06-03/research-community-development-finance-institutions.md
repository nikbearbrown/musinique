### SECTION 7: Community Development Finance Institutions (CDFIs)
**Core Claim:** CDFIs fill the gap between mainstream bank lending and exclusion, offering loans and support to micro-enterprises, sole traders, and businesses in underserved communities and geographies.

**Supporting Evidence:**
- 60 active CDFIs operating across all UK regions
- 2011/12: 13,000 enquiries from businesses, requesting £231M total; enquiries from 10-500 employee businesses up 150% year-on-year
- £30M commitment from Regional Growth Fund, matched by £30M from Co-operative Bank and Unity Trust Bank in October 2012
- Fredericks Foundation: average loan £4,000+; expects to double lending to 200+ loans in the following 12 months

**Logical Gaps:**
- Demand (£231M requested) vs. supply (£60M newly committed via Regional Growth Fund announcement) reveals a structural underfunding gap the guide does not name explicitly. 13,000 enquiries requesting £231M against a newly announced £60M fund suggests most applicants will not receive funding.
- Average loan size at Fredericks Foundation (just over £4,000) is extremely modest capital for most business development activities. The guide presents this as a viable funding route without establishing what £4,000 can realistically accomplish.

**Methodological Soundness:** The CDFI section is among the guide's most honest about the structural supply constraints facing underserved borrowers. The gap between demand and supply is implicit in the data even if not explicitly stated.
