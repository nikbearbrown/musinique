### What the Book Does Not Prove

- That the cerebellum's role in musical emotion is specifically causal rather than correlational (the evidence is associative)
- That the 10,000-hour rule applies uniformly across domains and individuals
- That sexual selection is the primary evolutionary mechanism for music (the evidence supports it as *a* mechanism, not *the* mechanism)
- That expressivity—the soul of musical performance—has any tractable scientific explanation
