### INTERVIEW STRUCTURE (Applies Across All 13 Chapters)
Each interview follows a consistent question set: origin story, creativity definition, physical environment, subconscious role, discipline, co-writing, opening lines, imagery, writer's block, fear of failure, survival tips.

**Logical Method:** Comparative anecdote — thirteen practitioners responding to the same stimuli. The consistency of the question set enables cross-interview comparison.

**Structural Gap:** The interview format is conversational, not analytical. Claims made by interviewees are not challenged, tested against evidence, or compared to counter-examples. Every assertion is presented as equally valid regardless of its logical structure. This is the book's most significant methodological limit.
