### What the Book Proves

With reasonable confidence:
- Music recruits widespread and functionally specialized neural systems, including ancient subcortical structures and modern cortical ones
- Musical expectation is tracked automatically by the brain and involves overlapping but distinguishable neural systems from language
- Non-musicians encode musical details (pitch, tempo) in long-term memory with greater accuracy than previously thought
- Infant music perception is sophisticated, culturally sensitive, and shows processing advantages for consonant intervals and unequal-step scales
- The brain's reward system (nucleus accumbens, dopamine) responds to music, likely mediated by expectation dynamics
