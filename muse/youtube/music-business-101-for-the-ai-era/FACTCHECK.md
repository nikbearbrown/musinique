## MEDIUM confidence (article-sourced — not independently verified)

- **Streaming revenue arc:** US $14.6B (1999 peak) → roughly half for 16
  years → $15.9B new high in 2022–2023. Per the article's summary of Passman.
  The article itself flags Passman's $84/year vs $72/year per-subscriber
  comparison as his own rough research — treat directionally, not precisely.
- **Zero-sum streaming pool** framing: the article's analytic gloss on
  pro-rata mechanics. Mechanically sound, rhetorically sharpened.
- **Team economics:** manager 15–20% of gross; the $100K gross / $45K net /
  manager-takes-a-third worked example; business managers need no California
  credentials; agent 10% standard. Per the article's summary of Passman.
- **100,000 tracks uploaded daily** to streaming services. Per the article
  (Passman, at time of writing).
- **Recoupment worked example:** $2M album revenue leaving the artist $20K
  unrecouped while owing the producer $40K. Per the article — presented as
  Passman's illustrative arithmetic, which is how the film presents it.
- **Options history:** Newton-John (injunction limited to stated term),
  Dean Martin (delivery-based term ran indefinitely), Zappa (delivered all
  albums at once). Per the article's summary; standard industry lore.
- **360 deals:** 15–20% typical, 7.5–10% on touring. Per the article.
- **Statutory mechanical rate:** $0.12 or 2.31 cents/minute (as of Jan 2023).
  Per the article; rates are set by the Copyright Royalty Board and change.
- **French CNM study (Feb 2021):** per-user allocation cuts top-10 artists
  12–17%, adds ~10 euros/year for artists below the top 10,000. Per the
  article's citation.
- **31-second stream threshold / bot dilution mechanics:** per the article's
  summary of Passman's AI-dilution discussion.
