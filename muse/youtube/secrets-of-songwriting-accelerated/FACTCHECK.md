*The Secrets of Songwriting* by Susan Tucker (2003, Allworth Press).

**Method note:** All book/writer claims below are grounded in that article.

  interviewed. Article front-matter + introduction. CONFIRMED.

  hundreds of number one singles and tens of millions of records sold."

  CONFIRMED.

## Deliberate omissions / honesty notes

- The article itself flags: survivorship bias (only successful writers
  interviewed); all thirteen writers white, mostly male; 2003 industry
  context (pre-streaming). The narration does not repeat these caveats
  (slate-cut runtime), but nothing in the narration contradicts them.
- The 5/10-year Nashville timeline (Beavers) is presented in the article as
  "heard it said" wisdom with survivorship concerns — OMITTED from narration.
- Sillers's "financial comfort harms creativity" claim is flagged by the
  article as having zero empirical support — OMITTED.
- Wiseman's "writer's block is nonsense" contradicts other writers in the
  book — OMITTED (narration sides with the majority: the voice is real,
  write anyway).
- Cannon's Harlan Howard "What gives you the right?" exchange and Bell's
  foreword were cut for runtime, not for accuracy.
