# FACTCHECK.md — Film 05: "The Curator's Playbook: Stop Thinking Like an Artist"

Checked 2026-10-04 against the source article
(`articles/musinique-188014429-spotify-the-curator-s-playbook.md`, published
2026-02-15). Confidence levels are honest; MEDIUM items are article-sourced,
not independently verified. The article is itself a critical analysis of
Beckham "Batushi" Batushi's playbook — its caveats are carried over here.

## Article-sourced (MEDIUM — not independently verified)

- **Batushi's story:** released 150+ songs, built an expensive studio, got no
  traction; mindset shift was the turning point.
- **"Mood Lifting Happy Songs"** playlist: grew from 957 → 2,500+ followers.
- **Metrics:** 374 monthly listeners, 3.83 average streams per listener
  (≈1,434 streams/month). Math checks: 374 × 3.83 ≈ 1,432.
- **Zix Music:** commissioned a remix based on curation success. NOTE: the
  article flags that the book's conclusion conflates this remix commission
  with a "label deal" — the film says "commissioned remix," not "record deal."
- **Gapfiller philosophy:** "Don't ask what's popular, ask what's missing."
  Article-sourced as Batushi's stated philosophy.
- **8-of-100 rule:** 8% self-promotion ratio presented as Batushi's rule.
  The article notes this ratio was never A/B tested.
- **Golden positions** (2, 4, 6, 9, 20, 28, 48, 85 of 100): Batushi's claim,
  from "analyzing thousands of streams." The article finds NO methodology —
  no controls, no sample size, no way to isolate position from song quality.
  The film presents these as *claimed*, then explicitly doubts them (B07, B13).
- **Rotation system:** Top 8 anchors + 2 challengers. The article identifies
  an internal contradiction: challengers tested in non-golden positions
  (e.g., 15, 60) while anchors hold golden ones — a handicapped test. The
  film states this catch (B07).
- **$300/month budget** (SubmitHub ads + Instagram + TikTok/tools).
  Article-sourced; conversion data (spend → followers → streams) not provided.
- **Southworth/Greenwood comparisons** (7,000 listeners, 3x repeat rate,
  $500–600/month algorithm threshold): per the article, not verified here.

## Computed in the film (checkable arithmetic)

- **$4.30/month:** 1,434 streams × ~$0.003/stream. Rough per-stream rate is
  widely cited for Spotify; the film says "roughly," "about."
- **8 songs vs. 8+2:** the article notes Batushi's own math (8 anchors +
  2 challengers = 10) contradicts the "8 of 100" claim. Not raised in
  narration; noted here.

## Deliberate framing (not factual claims)

- **AI beats (B11–B12):** the film's own advice-synthesis — "AI compresses
  the trial part to an afternoon," pitch drafting, rotation analytics.
  Phrased as recommendations, no AI product names/prices asserted.
- "Ten thousand listeners who play you once are a puddle" — rhetorical.
- "One person can run a whole curator operation before lunch" — aspirational
  framing, not a measured claim.
