# FACTCHECK.md — Film 09: "Your AI Manager"

Checked 2026-10-05 against the source article
(`articles/musinique-189322198-music-the-business.md` — Ann Harrison,
*Music: The Business*, 4th ed., 2008, via the Musinique analytical review).
Confidence levels are honest; MEDIUM items are article-sourced, not
independently verified.

## HIGH confidence (case law, well documented)

- **Gilbert O'Sullivan (1985):** Court of Appeal voided management/recording/
  publishing contracts; declined to return copyrights — only future rights freed.
- **Joan Armatrading (1985):** five-year exclusive contract with 25%
  commission on new deals set aside for undue influence / lack of independent
  advice.
- **Elton John v. Dick James (1991):** fiduciary duty extends to companies
  under the manager's control.
- **Matthew Fisher v. Gary Brooker (2006):** organist awarded 40% of the
  musical copyright in "A Whiter Shade of Pale," ~39 years after the 1967
  recording.
- **Frankie Goes To Hollywood trade mark case:** band name owned by all
  members as partnership goodwill; Holly Johnson could not register it
  individually.
- **Saxon case:** first to register the trade mark wins absent a written
  agreement.
- **Macaulay v. Schroeder (1974):** exclusive publishing deal voided as
  unreasonable restraint of trade.
- **George Michael Wembley curfew fine:** £130,000 for overrunning the
  licensing curfew (£10,000/minute) — per the article.
- **20% management commission** presented in the article as the standard
  average.
