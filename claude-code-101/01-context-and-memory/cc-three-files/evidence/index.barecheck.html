Thursday Study Group Sign-Up

A weekly co-working hour for focused, quiet study.

We meet every Thursday evening to sit down together and work through what we're each learning. Bring whatever you're studying — a textbook, a problem set, a language you're picking up, notes for an exam. The first twenty minutes are for stating your goal for the session; the rest of the time is heads-down and quiet, with a short break in the middle.

Anyone is free to come. Sign up below so we know how many chairs to set out and can send you a reminder the morning of.
